Cost

How much does Google Ads cost in Malaysia?

There are two parts: your ad spend, which you pay directly to Google, and the management fee for running the account.

Click prices depend on your industry. In Malaysia many SME searches cost under RM5 a click, while competitive services can cost RM20 or more. For example, Google's own Keyword Planner shows advertisers bidding up to about RM250 a click to appear first for "google ads malaysia".

We'll show you the expected click prices for your searches before you commit to a budget, and you can start small and grow what works.

You stay the owner of your Google Ads account and your data, always.
Ads or SEO?

When Google Ads makes sense.

Ads are best when you need enquiries quickly, have a limited-time offer, or want to test which services and messages people respond to.

They are less useful when the budget is very small or the searches are too broad. In those cases, we'll tell you, and suggest starting with SEO instead.

The strongest setup is often both: ads for speed, SEO for long-term free traffic, with what the ads teach us shaping the website.

Questions

Good to know.

What is the minimum budget for Google Ads?

There's no fixed minimum from Google. We'll recommend a starting budget based on your click prices, so you get enough data to learn from within the first month.

How soon will I get enquiries?

Ads can show the same day they are approved. The first month is about learning which searches and messages bring real enquiries, then we cut waste and scale what works.

Do I own the Google Ads account?

Yes. The account is set up in your name and you keep full access. We work in it as a manager.

Do you run Facebook and Instagram ads too?

Our focus is search: Google and AI. If social ads suit your goals, we'll talk about it as part of the wider plan.

What's SEM?

Search engine marketing. In Malaysia it usually means paid search ads on Google, the ads at the top of search results.
