Why it matters

AI answers pick a few names. Is yours one of them?

When someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI Overview for a recommendation, they get a short answer with a handful of businesses, not ten blue links.

Those names come from what the AI can read and trust: your website, directories, reviews, news and forums. If that information is thin or inconsistent, a competitor gets named instead.

AI search is still new in Malaysia, so few businesses are working on it. That makes it one of the easier places to get ahead today.

GEO vs SEO

Same foundations, different finish line.

SEO aims for a high position on Google's results page. GEO aims to be named inside the AI's answer. Answer engine optimisation (AEO) is another name for much the same idea.

The foundations overlap: a fast, clear website, good structure and trusted mentions. That's why we treat AI search as part of every SEO project, not a separate product you have to buy twice.

Questions

Good to know.

What is generative engine optimisation (GEO)?

GEO is the work of making your business easy for AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI Overviews to find, understand and recommend in their answers.

Can you guarantee ChatGPT will mention my business?

No one can. AI answers change from one question to the next. We improve the signals AI tools rely on and track how often you are named, month by month.

What is llms.txt?

A simple text file at the root of your website that lists your key pages for AI tools. Support is still limited, so it helps a little; clear pages and trusted mentions matter more.

Does AI search replace SEO?

No. Google search is still the biggest source of visitors for most businesses, and AI tools draw on the same web pages. Good SEO is the base for good AI visibility.

How do you measure AI visibility?

We keep a fixed list of buyer questions and ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI each month, recording whether you're named, how you're described and who else appears.
