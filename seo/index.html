SEO price in Malaysia

What does SEO cost?

SEO pricing in Malaysia varies widely because the work varies widely. A ten-page local business needs far less than an online store with a thousand products.

The main things that set the price are: how many pages need work, how competitive your searches are, how much new content is needed, and whether we make the changes or your team does.

We start with the free scan, then propose a scope with clear deliverables and a price before any work starts. You'll know what you're paying for and why.

Be careful with SEO packages that promise "page 1 in 30 days". Nobody controls Google's rankings, and shortcuts can get a site penalised.

Questions

Good to know.

How long does SEO take?

Technical fixes and page rewrites can show movement within weeks. For competitive searches, expect three to six months of steady work before rankings and enquiries clearly grow.

Can you guarantee the #1 spot on Google?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Google decides rankings. We guarantee clear work, honest reporting and a plan based on evidence.

SEO or Google Ads: which should I start with?

SEO builds free traffic that lasts but takes time. Google Ads brings visitors from day one but stops when you stop paying. Many SMEs run ads for quick enquiries while SEO grows.

Do you handle Malay and Chinese searches?

Yes. Many Malaysians search in Malay or Chinese, and those searches are often less competitive. We check volume in all three languages.

Do I need a new website for SEO?

Usually not. Most sites can be improved as they are. We only suggest a rebuild if the current site truly holds you back.

What's included in a monthly SEO service?

Month 1 sets the foundations: audit, keyword map, technical fixes and tracking. After that, every month we improve or write pages, publish one helpful article, fix technical issues, track rankings and AI answers, and send a one-page report. See the full list.

Do you buy backlinks?

No. We never buy links or use private blog networks. We earn mentions through useful content, business listings and partners, which is safer and lasts.

If we stop working together, do we keep our rankings and content?

Yes. Everything we create is yours: pages, articles, accounts and data. Rankings depend on Google, but nothing is taken down when we leave.

How do I choose an SEO agency in Malaysia?

Ask three things: what exactly they do each month, how they build links, and how they report. Be careful with ranking guarantees and very cheap packages; good SEO is steady, visible work.

Does SEO help with ChatGPT and AI answers?

Yes. AI tools lean on the same signals: clear pages, structured data and trusted mentions. We cover this as part of the work. See AI search optimisation.
