How it works

No guesswork and no vanity rankings. Every recommendation is tied to real search demand in Malaysia.

1

Find the real searches

Keyword demand from Google's own Keyword Planner for Malaysia — in English, Bahasa Malaysia and Chinese — including the "how to" and "best … in Penang/KL" questions buyers ask.

2

Check where you stand

Your position on Google for each search, and whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity mention you when someone asks for a recommendation.

3

Price the gap in RM

Missed clicks × what Google Ads would charge for them. You see exactly what ranking higher is worth before spending anything.

4

Fix, then prove it

A short, ordered fix list (pages, titles, structured data, content). We re-scan monthly and show the before/after.

What one missed search is worth

The report uses one simple, transparent formula, so you can check every number yourself.

missed clicks = monthly searches × (click rate at position 3 − click rate at your position)
traffic value = missed clicks × Google Ads cost per click

Questions

Who is this for?

Malaysian small and medium businesses — especially B2B, services and high-ticket products — where one new customer from search is worth a lot.

What is "AI search" or GEO?

More buyers now ask ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews "what's the best … in Malaysia?". Generative engine optimisation (GEO) makes your business easy for those tools to find, trust and quote.

Do I need to run ads?

No. Ads cost is only used as a yardstick for what free search traffic is worth.

Do you need access to my accounts?

Not for the scan. It is done from the outside, like a customer would see you. Search Console access helps later for tracking.
