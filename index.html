Questions

Good to know.

Who do you work with?

SMEs building their first digital foundation, businesses improving an existing website, and teams looking for clearer direction across search and marketing.

What is AI search, or GEO?

More buyers now ask ChatGPT or Google's AI “what's the best … in Malaysia?”. Generative engine optimisation (GEO) makes your business easy for those tools to find, trust and recommend.

Is the free scan really free?

Yes. It's done from the outside, the way a customer sees you, so we don't need access to your accounts. If you'd like help with the fixes afterwards, we can talk about that.

Do I need to run ads?

No. SEM is there if paid search suits your goals, but many businesses start with their website and organic search.

How do we get started?

With a conversation. Tell us where your business is today and where you'd like it to go, and we'll suggest a useful first step.
